Privacy policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026.

Shirley OpenClaw is a private, personal-use assistant operated by the owner of its connected accounts. It is not a public service and does not accept public registrations.

Google data and its use

With the account owner's authorization, the assistant accesses data covered by the Google permissions granted to it. Depending on the enabled services and permissions, this may include email and attachments, files and document contents, calendars and events, contacts, tasks, forms, meetings, classroom information, and scripts. It may read, create, edit, send, or delete data when authorized to carry out the owner's requests and automations. Enabling an API alone does not grant access.

Processing and disclosure

Google data needed to fulfill a request may be processed on the operator's computer and sent to the AI model providers and other service providers configured for that request. Relevant content may appear in assistant conversations, logs, memory, or generated files. This information is not published on this website. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Data is shared only as necessary to provide the requested functionality, as directed by the owner, or as required by law.

Shirley OpenClaw's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored on the operator's system, separately from this public website and repository. Access is restricted through the host's access controls. Google API connections use encrypted HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and control

Tokens are retained until removed or revoked. Copies of data in conversations, logs, memory, and files may persist until deleted by the operator or under the relevant provider's retention settings. Revoking Google access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing copies. The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections and request deletion of locally stored tokens and copied data by contacting the operator.

This website

This static site uses no analytics, forms, or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement.
